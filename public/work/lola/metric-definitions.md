## Locked definitions

### 96.3% — did not require staff escalation

```text
Conversations that never entered a staff-help / human-reply path
÷ all valid guest conversations in the pilot window (n=380)
```

- **Not the same as:** resolved to customer satisfaction, order completed, or “closed successfully.”
- **Evidence note:** 14 of 380 threads escalated to staff.
- Prefer public wording: **“96.3% of conversations did not require staff escalation.”**
- Folder cards may label this as **Pilot signal** and say **“valid conversations”** when the denominator (n=380, spam/test/incomplete excluded) is defined in this note.
- Avoid: “closed without staff,” “96.3% resolution.”

### 97.7% — first bot reply under 2 minutes

```text
First Lola bot replies arriving in < 2 minutes
÷ first-reply sample on the live flyer thread (n=353)
```

- Median ~4 seconds (publish with n).
- Keep **staff reply latency** separate — do not blend into this metric.

### ~1,200 — weekly loyalty / flyer reach

- Families on the weekly deals blast list (approximate).
- Scale signal for why a single inbox cannot keep up at rush — not a MAU product metric.
