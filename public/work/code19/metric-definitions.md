## Locked definitions

### 1,500+ — new monthly sessions

```text
Additional monthly sessions after redesign vs pre-redesign baseline
(SEO + optimized direct channels)
```

- Prefer: **“1,500+ new monthly sessions.”**
- Avoid collapsing to bare “1,500+ monthly sessions” without “new.”

### −36% organic bounce (to 58.5%)

```text
(Organic bounce_pre − organic bounce_post) / organic bounce_pre
Post organic bounce = 58.5%
```

- Always publish the destination rate with the delta.

### Direct session length — 3.6 min

- Average session duration for **direct** traffic post-redesign.
- Organic averaged ~96 seconds — keep channels separate; do not blend.

### Seed $150K → $500K — concurrent, not sole cause

- Elevating digital brand presence **supported** seed credibility.
- Prefer: **“Seed rose $150K→$500K alongside stronger brand presence — not claimed as sole cause.”**
- Metric label in case study: **“Seed (concurrent).”**
- Do not imply a controlled website A/B caused the round.
